Returnable Pallet Tracking — How It Works

Every AIS pallet tracked by ID from plant to customer and back · Management overview · v0.4 · Oct 2026
Problem todayDispatch is scanned, but returns are counted by quantity from a manual challan. Pallets at customers are not known by ID, so losses and aging are invisible.
What changesOne barcode tag per pallet, scanned at four points. A Return Slip with QR replaces the manual challan. Oracle and GCS are integrated so there is no double entry.
ResultLive plant availability, customer-wise holding by pallet ID, automatic reminders, chargeable list for overdue pallets, full history for every pallet and tag.
Plant availabilityExact count of pallets ready to dispatch, per plant, live.
Customer holdingWhich pallets each customer has and for how many days.
Auto remindersMail to customer when holding exceeds the agreed limit.
Zero double entrySale order to EBS, challan from EBS or app, GCS in both directions.
Full traceabilityPallet history, tag change history, exception log for audit.
